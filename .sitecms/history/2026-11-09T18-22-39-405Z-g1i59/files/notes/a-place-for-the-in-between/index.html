From the notebook

A place for the in-between

A welcome note about the ideas, experiments and half-finished thoughts that belong here.

Field notes10 October 20263 min read

Some things are too small for a tutorial and too interesting to leave in a private notebook. This is a home for those things: what I am trying, what caught my attention, and what I think after spending a little time with it.

Expect short notes alongside longer pieces. A post might begin with a tool, a book, a question, or a tiny change to the way I work. The useful part is the thinking that follows.

What you will find here

There will be experiments in progress, considered opinions, and occasional recommendations when something has earned one. I want to be clear about what I have tested, what I am still unsure of, and when my mind changes.

For detailed technical guides, I also write at TechArticles. This space is looser by design: a place to follow curiosity without needing every thought to become a full how-to.

A notebook is allowed to show the thinking, not just the conclusion.

If something here sparks a thought of your own, get in touch. The best notes tend to lead to better conversations.
