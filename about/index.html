A little introduction

About this notebook

A place for the thoughts that happen between the big projects.

My Notes from Today is a personal blog about what I am exploring, trying and thinking about. Some entries will be quick observations; others will take the time to work through an idea.

The common thread is curiosity. I want to share what I learn while an idea is still alive, including the questions and changes of mind along the way.

Here and elsewhere

My more detailed technical writing lives at TechArticles. This notebook gives me room to write more freely about tools, projects and the things that catch my attention.

If a note starts a conversation, I would love to hear from you. Send a message.
