Black Friday Is Coming, But My Wishlist Is Smaller Than Ever

A welcome note about the ideas, experiments and half-finished thoughts that belong here.

November 9, 2026

It's coming up to Black Friday again, and I've realised that this year I probably have the smallest wishlist I've ever had.

In previous years, I've usually had a fairly long list of tools, plugins and services I was hoping would go on offer. This year feels a little different. Maybe it's because I've already built up a decent toolkit, or perhaps I'm just getting a little more selective about what I actually need.

That's not to say I won't be keeping an eye on what's available.

There are still a few gaps in my toolkit I'd like to fill, particularly around WordPress. Things like media offloading and multiple domain support are on my radar, but beyond those, I don't really have anything specific in mind. I think it'll largely depend on what comes up and whether it genuinely adds something useful to what I already have.

Something I've been thinking about more recently is how important it is to support the people behind the products we use.

The market isn't particularly easy at the moment, and I do wonder how many good independent developers and smaller software businesses are finding things tough. We all love a bargain, myself included, but there's also something to be said for supporting people who are building good products, maintaining them and continuing to improve them.

I'd much rather spend money on something genuinely useful, from someone doing good work, than buy another tool simply because the discount looks impressive.

I'll probably share a few offers that catch my attention along the way, not because I'm looking to promote everything under the sun, but because sometimes a genuinely good deal is worth mentioning.

And, of course, there's always that lingering thought about the deals you didn't take.

I still regret not picking up FluentCRM all those years ago. At the time, I wasn't sure I needed it. Looking back, it would probably have been one of the better purchases I could have made.

But I suppose that's the balancing act with Black Friday. Knowing when something is a genuinely good opportunity and when you're simply being tempted by a discount.

This year, I'm going into it with fewer expectations, a much shorter list and perhaps a little more appreciation for the people who make the tools we rely on.

What about you?

Is there anything you're particularly hoping to pick up this Black Friday, or are you, like me, finding that your wishlist is getting smaller each year?
