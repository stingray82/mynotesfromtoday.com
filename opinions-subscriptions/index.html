Opinions & Subscriptions

A welcome note about the ideas, experiments and half-finished thoughts that belong here.

November 1, 2026

I seem to be in my grump old man phase this week, but I woke up this morning and culled about $1500 worth of subscriptions. Why? I am spending to much on stuff I am not using or the people who create the products I don't agree with on a fundamental basis

I am spending more and more time reviewing what people are putting out there and there is a massive shift going on there are developers, founders and creators I like and respect and they will continue to get my money both for good products and for support but equally there has been some that see the AI emerging world as there way to push there agenda or wrong there customers

I am not out here vibe coding replacements but I already have dozens of plugins, SAAS and Lifetime deals and I think its time I used some of them even if there not perfect, I think as AI makes things easier and working different its even more important for me to vote with my feet.

I moved my Helpdesk, docs, changelogs and roadmaps over to CoreSupport a few weeks ago I don't need 3 different backups so if it was a subscription it has gone.

You've posted stuff I don't like, I don't agree with and you continually annoy or belittle your customers for what ever reason, I don't need confrontational marketing it doesn't work on me your gone.

It doesn't matter how good your products are or how ingrained they where its worth the effort, I think I just woke up this morning and thought I couldn't just ignore what has been going on and continues to go on.

Now in the grand scheme of things no one is going to notice me disappearing but I feel better no longer supporting people I don't like and getting rid of subscriptions I've kept for convenience rather than use.

Do I feel better No, is it the right ethical decision absolutely am I done? nope!
